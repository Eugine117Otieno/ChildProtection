Protect Children From Harm

Understand the dangers children face and how we can prevent them.

Types of Child Abuse

🚫

Physical Abuse

Hurting a child physically through hitting, kicking, burning or other actions that cause pain or injury.

💔

Emotional Abuse

Constantly threatening, insulting, humiliating, rejecting or frightening a child.

⚠️

Sexual Abuse

Any sexual contact, activity or exploitation involving a child. A child is never responsible for the abuse.

🍽️

Neglect

Failing to provide essential food, clothing, healthcare, shelter, education or supervision.

⛔

Child Exploitation

Using children for forced labour, trafficking, harmful activities or other forms of exploitation.

📱

Online Abuse

Cyberbullying, online grooming, threats, harassment or misuse of children's images and information.

Warning Signs to Notice

01

Unexplained injuries or repeated bruises.

02

Sudden fear of a particular person or place.

03

Changes in behaviour, sleep or school attendance.

04

Signs of neglect, hunger or lack of essential care.

05

Withdrawal, anxiety or unusual distress.

Warning signs do not prove abuse, but they should be taken seriously.

Protect. Report. Support.

Every adult has a role in protecting children.
