You Are Not Alone

If you or another child is in danger, speak up and seek help.

Where Can You Get Help?

👨‍👩‍👧

Trusted Adults

Talk to a trusted parent, guardian, relative or responsible adult who can help keep you safe.

🏫

Your School

Speak to a teacher, school counsellor or child protection teacher.

👮

Child Protection Officers

Contact child protection authorities or police if a child is being abused or is in danger.

🏥

Health Professionals

Visit a health facility if a child needs medical treatment or emotional support.

🇰🇪 Kenya Child Helpline

If a child needs protection, support or advice in Kenya, contact the national Child Helpline.

📞 116

Child Helpline – 116

Free child protection support.
