Every Child Has Rights

Every child deserves dignity, respect, protection and opportunities.

Fundamental Rights of Children

📚

Right to Education

Every child has the right to learn, attend school and develop their talents.

🏥

Right to Health

Children have the right to medical care, nutritious food and clean water.

🏠

Right to Shelter

Children deserve a safe place to live and caring people around them.

🛡️

Right to Protection

Children must be protected from violence, abuse, neglect and exploitation.

🗣️

Right to Expression

Children have the right to express their views and be listened to.

🤝

Right to Equality

Every child deserves equal opportunities regardless of background or disability.

👨‍👩‍👧

Right to Family

Children deserve care, love and support from responsible adults.

⚽

Right to Play

Children deserve time to rest, play, enjoy recreation and participate in activities.

Rights Are Not Privileges

Every child deserves a safe, happy and healthy childhood.
