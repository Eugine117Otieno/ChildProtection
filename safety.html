Safety Tips for Children

Knowledge, confidence and awareness can help children stay safer.

Stay Safe Every Day

01

Always tell a trusted adult when you feel unsafe or frightened.

02

You have the right to say NO to unwanted touching or unsafe activities.

03

Never keep secrets about someone hurting, threatening or exploiting you.

04

Stay with trusted people when travelling or attending unfamiliar places.

05

Do not share passwords, personal details or private pictures online.

06

Block and report people who threaten, bully or make you uncomfortable online.

07

Ask for help if someone pressures you to do something you do not feel comfortable doing.

08

Remember: abuse is never your fault. You deserve protection and support.

Online Safety

🔐

Protect Your Passwords

Use strong passwords and never share them with strangers.

📵

Think Before Sharing

Do not send private photos or personal information to people you do not trust.

🚨

Report Online Abuse

Save evidence safely and tell a trusted adult if someone threatens or harasses you online.

Your Safety Matters

Be brave. Speak up. Seek help. You are never alone.
